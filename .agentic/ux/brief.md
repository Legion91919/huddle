# UX brief: stokvel group screen

Users:       stokvel members (weekly, on a phone, often on patchy mobile data) and treasurers (weekly, need to prove totals); low to medium digital confidence.
Main job:    see the group balance you can trust, and pay this month's contribution safely (never twice) in under 30 seconds.
Data:        group name, balance (sum of ledger entries), this month's paid count and amount collected, members (name, role, paid this month), contribution history (date, member, amount, balance after), my limit and wallet.
Tone:        calm, trustworthy, plain language; one teal accent; amounts in ZAR with tabular numbers, right-aligned.
Must avoid:  more than one accent colour, red/green as the only signal, decorative charts, jargon such as "idempotency" in the main copy.
